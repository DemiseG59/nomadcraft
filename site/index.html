About

What this server is

Nomadcraft is a survival SMP for friends and friends of friends, running a Fabric modpack with extra mobs, magic and pets. It's strictly survival, PvP is friendly and opt-in, and the economy runs on diamonds through a Hermitcraft-style shopping district. We usually play from around 10 PM onward, and the whole thing is filmed for YouTube.
