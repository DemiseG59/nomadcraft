About

What this server is

Nomadcraft is a survival SMP that's open to new players, running a Fabric modpack with extra mobs, magic and pets. It's strictly survival, PvP is friendly and opt-in, and the economy runs on diamonds through a player-run shopping district. Hop on and join us.

01
No griefing or stealing

Don't break, take or blow up anything that isn't yours.

02
Pranks are fine if reversible

Keep it funny. Never wreck someone's build or farm.

03
No PvP outside events

Fighting only happens in opted-in events and wars.

04
Ask before building nearby

Check with a player before you build close to their base.

05
No cheats, no x-ray

Strictly survival. Nobody goes into creative.

06
Mind the lag, talk it out

Keep farms reasonable. Sort disputes yourselves first; Demise steps in if needed.
